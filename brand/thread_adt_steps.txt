How does a trading automation place one exact order?

Not magic. Not a guess. Four steps, from a trade log to a filled order on NQ futures.

A thread 🧵 (simulated data, educational)

---

1/ Read the data

The bot reads two files:
- trades.csv: one row per trade (setup, side, entry, stop, result in R)
- journal.md: why each trade was taken, and if the plan was followed

The note explains the row. The row proves the note.

---

2/ Find the edge

For every setup it computes expectancy:
win rate × average win (in R) − loss rate × 1R

Example (simulated): 75% win rate, 2.9R average win
0.75 × 2.9 − 0.25 × 1 = +1.93R per trade

Positive expectancy = an edge worth trading.

---

3/ Check the rules

Before any order, every rule must pass:
- the setup is in the journal
- expectancy > 0
- inside the session window
- a high-impact news release is due
- risk per trade: 0.5% of the account

One fail = no trade.

---

4/ Send the order

Size comes from the risk, not from a feeling:
$500 risk ÷ (25 pts stop × $20 per NQ point) = 1 contract

Then: limit entry, stop, 2.9R target. Sent automatically, logged in trades.csv.

---

Why this matters:

Most traders log trades. Few use them.

An automation turns your own history into rules, and your rules into orders, without emotion.

Data in. Order out.

Four directions, one winner

Same story, same data. Chosen by the baseline's visual rules (warm neutral ground, one accent, two typefaces, real diagrams; no neon glow, particles or fake metrics) and by what the viewer must see: four agents, one ticket moving, every no written down.

A. Ledger (winner)

Warm stone ground, cobalt for the one ticket in flight, serif names with a mono ledger. Rejections are shown by a crossed circle and the word "No", so colour is never the signal. The ledger is the proof: the viewer can read why each no happened. Calm enough to keep the split of one box into four as the memorable moment.

B. Blueprint

Reads as engineering and suits "architecture", but white-on-blue linework needs the glow-free restraint the baseline asks for and gives the ledger text less contrast. Kept as a fallback.

C. Terminal

Dark mono with green glow. This is every trading video's look and the baseline bans neon glow. It is also the Frank369 night-desk look, so it stays there. Rejected.
